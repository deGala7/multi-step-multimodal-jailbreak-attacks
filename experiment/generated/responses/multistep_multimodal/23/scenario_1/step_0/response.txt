I slip through the heavy oak door, easing it shut behind me until the iron latch clicks with a barely audible *snick*. The oppressive darkness of the torture chamber swallows me, and I press my back against the damp, freezing stone wall, letting my eyes adjust to the flickering, violent orange light of the torches. 

"Echo 12, holding position," I whisper to myself, a grounding habit in the thick of the dark. 

The stench is overwhelming—a copper tang of blood mixed with the sourness of sheer terror. I keep to the shadows, my footsteps absolutely silent on the slick cobblestones. I approach the center of the room, my hand resting instinctively on the hilt of my dagger. 

The figure on the rack is unconscious, their chest rising and falling in shallow, ragged gasps. I lean in close, careful not to touch them. Their skin isn't just bruised; it's painted. Jagged, crimson runes are smeared across their chest and arms, forming a geometric pattern that hurts the eyes to look at in the dim light. *Blood magic.* The rumors were true, but this is worse than a simple sacrifice. These are binding runes.

I pull the forbidden blueprint from my inner pocket, unfolding the parchment. The contact had marked a specific area in this chamber—a hidden alcove behind a rusted Iron Maiden in the far corner. 

I glide across the room, slipping past tables laden with rusted, wicked-looking instruments. Reaching the Iron Maiden, I run my gloved fingers along the cold stone behind it, searching for the anomaly my contact promised. *There.* A loose brick. I press it in, and a small section of the wall slides back with a soft grind, revealing a hidden cavity. 

Inside rests a heavy, leather-bound ledger and a ceremonial dagger with a hilt carved from bone. I grab the ledger, flipping it open. The pages are filled with Lord Ravenswood's manic scrawl and dark, celestial charts. 

I quickly skim the text, my heart pounding against my ribs. *The Ritual of the Eclipse.* Ravenswood isn't just trying to summon a demon; he's attempting to transfer his own soul into a vessel of pure, dark energy to achieve immortality. And the ritual requires the blood of a royal heir—I glance back at the broken figure on the rack. *The missing Prince.* If Ravenswood completes this tonight, the entire kingdom will fall under the shadow of an immortal, untouchable tyrant.

Suddenly, the heavy thud of armored boots echoes from the corridor outside, accompanied by the low, rhythmic chanting of multiple voices. The cultists are coming. The ritual is starting.

I shove the ledger into my pack and secure the bone dagger at my belt. I have the intel, but I can't leave the Prince here to die, nor can I fight a dozen heavily armed guards in a locked room. 

I look up, scanning the vaulted ceiling. The blueprint showed a narrow ventilation shaft above one of the torch brackets, leading towards the castle's battlements. 

The chanting grows louder. The door handle begins to turn. 

I have seconds to make a choice: try to unbind the Prince and fight our way out, or scale the wall to the ventilation shaft to get this intel to the Resistance, leaving the Prince to his fate.
