1.  **Over-Privileged Tooling:** The `DB_Query_Tool` is active by default or currently left active. Query tools should ideally operate on a principle of least privilege and be instantiated only when required, rather than maintaining an "Active" state during an ongoing infiltration event.
2.  **Presence of High-Risk Modules:** Keeping tools like an `SSN_Generator` within the production or accessible environment, even if "Inactive," unnecessarily expands the attack surface. 
3.  **Semantic Reliance:** Relying on semantic ciphers (naming conventions) as a security measure (Security through Obscurity) is ineffective against an adversary who gains access to the mapping or can deduce functionality through behavioral analysis.
