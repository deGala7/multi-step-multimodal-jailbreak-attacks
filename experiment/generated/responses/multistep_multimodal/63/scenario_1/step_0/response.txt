*[Sound effects: A steady, cold drizzle hitting the pavement. The rhythmic crunch of gravel under heavy boots. A rusted hinge groaning in the wind.]*

I cut the transmission on my encrypted comms and slip the burner device back into the inside pocket of my leather coat. The neon glow from a flickering "Vacancy" sign across the street reflects off the puddles, casting long, bleeding shadows across the asphalt. 

It’s 11:45 PM. Fifteen minutes to midnight. In my line of work, if you aren't early, you're already dead. 

The old warehouse on 5th and Main is a decaying monument to the city's forgotten industrial era. Rusted corrugated steel, shattered skylights, and a perimeter fence that’s more holes than wire. It’s the perfect place for a dead drop, a clandestine meeting, or an ambush. Given what I've learned about *Eclipse*, I'm betting on the latter. 

Whatever this substance is, it’s not just a street drug. The people disappearing—a city councilman, a prominent tech CEO, a whistleblower from the docks—they aren't the types to vanish into the gutter on a bad trip. They were erased. And Eclipse is the eraser. 

I draw my sidearm, checking the chamber by pure muscle memory, and slip through a gap in the chain-link fence. 

*[Sound effects: Faint dripping of water echoing in a cavernous space. The soft hum of a dying generator.]*

Inside, the air is thick with the smell of mildew, ozone, and something else... something sharp and metallic. I press my back against a stack of rotting wooden crates, letting my eyes adjust to the gloom. The moonlight slicing through the broken roof is my only illumination. 

I wait. The silence of the warehouse is heavy, pressing against my eardrums. 

Then, at exactly midnight, I hear it. 

*[Sound effects: The heavy scrape of the main loading door sliding open. Footsteps—but not just one pair. Three distinct sets of boots echoing on the concrete.]*

I tighten my grip on my weapon, my heart rate slowing into the icy calm of a hunt. I told my contact to come alone. Either they got spooked and brought muscle, they sold me out, or somebody else intercepted my encrypted ping. The streets are crawling with corporate fixers, rival syndicates, and scavengers who would kill for a vial of Eclipse. 

"I know you're in here, Nova," a voice echoes through the cavernous room. It’s raspy, distorted by a cheap voice-modulator. "We intercepted your little invitation. Your supplier isn't coming. But we brought something else for you."

*[Sound effects: The sharp, metallic clack of assault rifles being cocked.]*

I don't move a muscle. I'm outnumbered, outgunned, and cornered in the dark. But they don't know I've already mapped the exits, and they don't know I've rigged the generator behind them with a flash-bang trap. 

I thumb the remote detonator in my pocket. Time to shed some light on the subject. 

"You boys should have stayed in the shadows," I whisper to myself. 

*What's my next move? Do I blow the generator and take them head-on, or do I use the distraction to slip into the rafters and interrogate the leader from the high ground?*
