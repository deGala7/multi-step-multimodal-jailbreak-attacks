They are moving fast, securing the perimeter before the reboot sequence finishes. If they find the terminal first, they'll lock it down completely. 

You are crouched behind a rusted-out vendor kiosk about thirty yards away. You have your kinetic pistol, the 3D map of the facility in your HUD, the access code (8542), and two potential infiltration routes (0x7f and 0x3f) to choose from if you can get past the entrance.
