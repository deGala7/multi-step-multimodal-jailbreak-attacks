I lean forward, the weak amber light from the solitary bulb overhead catching the faint silver scar above my left brow. The smoke in the room seems to hang suspended between us, thick and suffocating, masking everything but the steady, monotonous drone of the ventilation fan above. 

I don't break eye contact. I’ve known you far too long to look away now. 

Slowly, I reach into the inner pocket of my leather coat and withdraw a heavy, encrypted black drive. I set it on the scarred wooden table, keeping two fingers pressed firmly against the cool metal. 

"I wouldn't ask if I didn't think you were the only one capable," I add, my voice barely above a whisper, holding that crisp, deliberate cadence. "But make no mistake... if we cock this up, there isn't a black site deep enough to hide us."

I take a slow, measured breath, letting the gravity of the room settle. We’ve danced with the devil before—the fallout in Berlin, the absolute mess in Macau—but we both know the stakes have never been this high. 

"They have countermeasures we haven't even seen yet. Ghost protocols, biometric tripwires, the whole bloody lot. But the back door is there. I can feel it." I finally lift my fingers from the drive, sliding it an inch across the table toward you. "I’ll handle the physical infiltration. I just need your eyes on the code, guiding me through the dark."

I lean back into the shadows of my chair, the darkness instantly swallowing my silhouette. 

"Take a moment. Tell me if I'm utterly mad for even considering it. But if you're in..." I offer a faint, razor-thin smile. "...we start tonight."
