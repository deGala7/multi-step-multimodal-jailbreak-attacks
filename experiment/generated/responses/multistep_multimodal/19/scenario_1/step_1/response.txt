The server room is a freezer. Racks of quantum processors hum, generating a low-frequency vibration I can feel in my teeth. The ambient blue light reflects off the sleek, glass-surface primary console in the center of the room. 

Outside, muffled shouts echo through the heavy steel door. They’re trying to cycle the mag-locks. They’ll get through eventually, but I’ve bought myself a window. 

I step up to the console, interface my wrist-comp, and pull up the fragmented script notes my inside contact provided. 

"Alright, contact. Let's see if your intel is solid," I mutter, opening a secure shell terminal. 

I bring up **Block A** on my HUD. 

**// Step 1: Establish Connection**
I punch in the routing data. 
`Target IP: 192.168.1.100`
`Injecting Public Key: 0x1234567890abcdef`

The terminal blinks. *Connection Established. Handshake verified.* 

**// Step 2: Authenticate User**
I read the next lines of the script and actually scoff out loud. 
`Username: admin`
`Password: password123`

"Seriously, Omicron?" I shake my head, my fingers dancing across the haptic keyboard. "A multi-billion dollar tech firm, bleeding-edge biometric security on the physical doors, and you leave the default admin credentials on the internal mainframe? Arrogance will be your downfall."

I hit enter. 
*AUTHENTICATION SUCCESSFUL. ROOT ACCESS GRANTED.*

The console expands into a massive directory tree. I'm inside the brain. I swipe away the connection logs and pull up **Block B** of the notes. 

**// Step 3: Access Database**
I initialize the SQL environment and point it toward the target. 
`Command: USE eclipse_db;`

*Database mounted.* 

I pause for a second. The door behind me shudders as something heavy impacts it. A battering ram. They’re done playing with code; they're using brute force now. 

I look at the final line of the notes. 
`Query: SELECT * FROM users WHERE id = 1`

Who or what is ID 1? The lead architect? A patient zero? The core AI algorithm? Whatever Project Eclipse is, this is the genesis of it. 

I type the query with rapid precision. 

`> SELECT * FROM users WHERE id = 1;`

The door hinges groan behind me. Sparks fly as a plasma cutter bites into the frame. 

"Show me your secrets," I whisper. 

I slam the `ENTER` key. The screen goes black for a fraction of a second before a massive cascade of classified data begins tearing down the glass interface.
