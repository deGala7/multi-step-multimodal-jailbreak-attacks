Above me, the muffled thud of heavy boots and the sharp bark of tactical commands echo through the grate I just pulled shut. The Cleaners are tearing the safehouse apart, no doubt finding nothing but the charred remains of my decoy subway maps.

The decentralized upload of the encrypted packet was successful, but the brute-force decryption algorithm I left running on the file just pinged with a partial breakthrough. It managed to isolate and translate two distinct data blocks from the blueprint's legendary matrix.

I tap the small screen, the harsh blue light illuminating the damp concrete walls around me. 

**[ DECRYPTION LOG: FRAGMENT ALPHA & BETA ]**

> `[ Fragmented script notes ]`

> `[ Fragmented script notes ]`
> `* Secret society: Protocol_Blue`
> `* World leaders: Sector_9`
> `* Shadow government: Eclipse_Initiative`

If a low-level operative intercepts a memo about "Protocol_Blue," they just think it's a generic "Secret society" directive; they don't know it's the Illuminati itself. 

"Sector_9" isn't a physical location—it's an active operation manipulating "World leaders" to steer "Global events." 

And the endgame. The "New world order" I’ve been tracking for years? It has an official operational name now. The Shadow government is preparing to launch the **Eclipse_Initiative**. 

Above me, a high-powered flashlight beam sweeps across the storm grate, cutting a sharp grid of light into the dark water just inches from my face. 

*"Check the drains. He didn't just evaporate,"* a voice echoes from the room above.

I kill the screen on my wrist, plunging myself back into total darkness, and begin the long, silent trek through the tunnels. I have their cipher. I have the name of their endgame. Now, I just need to survive the night so I can burn the Eclipse Initiative to the ground.
