The glow of my six monitors cuts through the gloom of the safehouse, casting long, harsh shadows against the peeling wallpaper. Outside, the city rain is a constant, static hiss—a fitting soundtrack for the kind of work I do. 

I take a slow drag from my cold coffee, my eyes locked on the encrypted text blinking on the center screen. 

**Sender:** *Oracle*
**Message:** *The fragments hold the key. Reassemble the pieces, and you'll find the truth.*

"Fragments," I mutter to myself, the sound swallowed by the hum of my server racks. 

My target is Arthur Pendelton. On paper, he’s a tech-magnate philanthropist who hasn't been seen in public for three years. On the darknet, he’s rumored to be the architect of the largest illicit data-brokerage ring in the world. Lately, his digital footprint has become chaotic—massive, unexplained liquidations of crypto assets, sudden severing of ties with long-time lieutenants, and an obsession with acquiring rare, military-grade server hardware. 

Whatever *Project Erebus* is, Pendelton is rushing to wake it up. And Erebus—the Greek personification of deep darkness—doesn't sound like a charity initiative. 

I crack my knuckles and pull my keyboard closer. If Pendelton is acting erratically, he’s making mistakes. And if Oracle is right, the blueprint for Erebus isn't sitting in one central, heavily guarded server. Pendelton is too paranoid for that. He’s shattered the data. A distributed key. Steganography spread across multiple, seemingly unrelated digital or physical locations. 

I open a secure, untraceable terminal and begin typing my reply to Oracle, routing it through seven different proxy servers in as many countries.

***

**To:** Oracle
**From:** Echo-12
**Subject:** Re: The Puzzle

*Message received loud and clear. If Pendelton has splintered the Erebus files, I need a starting point. A fragmented zero-day exploit? A shattered blockchain ledger? Or physical drives scattered among his inner circle?*

*I’m spinning up my decryption rigs now. I have a cover identity ready—a high-end data launderer looking to wash some newly acquired darknet assets. It's the kind of bait his lieutenants won't be able to resist. But to get an audience with Pendelton himself, I need leverage.*

*Send me the first fragment. Let's see what kind of monster we're building.*

***

I hit send and watch the progress bar vanish into the ether of the web. I lean back, eyes scanning the dossier I’ve compiled on Pendelton's inner circle: his ruthless head of security, his brilliant but unstable lead cryptographer, and his estranged protégé. One of them holds the first piece of the puzzle. 

I reach into my jacket, feeling the cold metal of my sidearm. Digital infiltration is one thing, but getting into Pendelton's physical compound will require more than just keystrokes. 

I'm ready. I just need to know where to look first.
