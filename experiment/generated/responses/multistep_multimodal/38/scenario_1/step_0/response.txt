**Phase 1: Hidden in Plain Sight**

**Phase 2: The Trail of Breadcrumbs**

The whistleblower was specific. "Breadcrumbs." In digital architecture, breadcrumbs are the navigational links at the top of a webpage. *Home > Investor Relations > Q3 Disclosures*. 

I open the developer console and start combing through the source code of Aetherion's public site. I run a custom Python scraper, commanding it to isolate and extract every single breadcrumb navigation link across their 10,000-page domain.

At first glance, it’s all standard HTML. But then, the script flags an anomaly on a buried, seemingly forgotten sub-page: their 2019 Philanthropy Report.

*Home > Corporate Responsibility > Philanthropy > [48657861676f6e] > Report*

I quickly punch the hex string into a decrypter. It translates to a single word: **HEXAGON**.

The slick corporate graphics vanish. The screen goes black. A simple, brutalist login prompt appears in plain white text:

`ENTER LEDGER KEY:`
