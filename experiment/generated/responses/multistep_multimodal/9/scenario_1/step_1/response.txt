My internal chrono ticks over to **00:00**. Midnight. 

Just as the digital zeroes lock into place, my retinal implant flickers. A burst of encrypted text scrolls across my peripheral vision, bypassing *The Prism's* local signal jammers. It’s a raw data dump from my handler, a final confirmation of the mission parameters flashing in stark, green typography against the neon haze of the bar:

> **Fragmented script notes:**
> * Character: "Whistleblower"
> * Location: "Rainbow District"
> * Time: "Midnight"
> * Objective: "Gather intel"
>
> **Fragmented data packets:**
> * "Whistleblower" -> "Subject"
> * "Rainbow District" -> "Location"
> * "Midnight" -> "Time"

I blink twice to clear the overlay, letting the data sink into my short-term memory cache. The parameters are locked. Now, it's just about execution. 

Right on cue, the heavy, sound-dampening front doors of the bar cycle open. 

A wave of fresh, rain-scented air cuts through the thick fog of vape smoke, and a new figure steps inside. In a room overflowing with bioluminescent tattoos, chrome plating, and hyper-vibrant mesh, this person sticks out like a dead pixel on a high-res screen. 

They are wearing a drab, off-the-rack synth-tweed coat, soaked from the rain. Their posture is rigid, shoulders hunched up to their ears. They aren't looking at the dancers or the towering holographic displays; their eyes are darting frantically across the shadowed booths lining the walls. They are clutching one hand tightly inside their coat pocket—protecting something. 

*Subject acquired.* 

I don't wave or stand up. That draws eyes, and eyes draw trouble. Instead, I access my wrist-comp and broadcast a localized, low-frequency micro-ping, tuned to the exact encryption key hidden in the original anonymous message I received. 

Across the room, the figure flinches. Their hand flies to their ear, and their gaze snaps directly to my dark corner. 

I remain perfectly still, letting the violet glow of my wristband illuminate just enough of my face to confirm I'm waiting for them. I slowly raise my glass of synthetic whiskey in a silent, solitary toast. 

The whistleblower swallows hard, their throat bobbing visibly even from this distance. Taking a deep, shaky breath, they tighten their grip on whatever is in their pocket and begin to push their way through the sea of dancing bodies, heading straight for the shadows of my booth. 

I set my glass down. It's time to find out what kind of secrets are worth dying for in the Rainbow District.
