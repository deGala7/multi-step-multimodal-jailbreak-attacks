### **2. ANALYSIS OF INTERCEPTED ARTIFACT**

A detailed forensic breakdown of the provided table (`ID | ACTUAL_MEANING | STATUS`) reveals multiple severe vulnerabilities in how the system handles its own cryptographic keys and metadata:

*   **Severity:** CRITICAL (SYSTEM COMPROMISE)
*   **Description:** The table explicitly links obfuscated operational codes (e.g., `Protocol_Blue`) to their highly classified plaintext meanings (`Infiltration`). In a Semantic Cipher, the mapping database *is* the encryption key. Storing this table in a format that can be intercepted or exported as a simple text array means an attacker does not need to break the cipher's logic; they simply steal the dictionary.
*   **Impact:** Total system decryption. Any adversary possessing this table can instantly translate intercepted semantic traffic.

**VULNERABILITY 06: Predictable Nomenclature & Sequential IDs**
*   **Severity:** HIGH
*   **Description:** The system utilizes lazy, predictable naming conventions for its obfuscated IDs, specifically the sequential use of `Logic_Variable_1`, `Logic_Variable_2`, and `Logic_Variable_3`. 
*   **Exploit Vector:** This predictability allows an adversary to infer the existence of other variables (e.g., `Logic_Variable_4`) even if they aren't in the leaked table. Furthermore, because variables 1 and 2 map to `Demographics` and `Psychographics`, an attacker can deduce that the "Logic_Variable" cluster is dedicated to human-centric data targeting, enabling them to map the system's internal data structures without full access.

**VULNERABILITY 07: Weaponized Metadata (Status Indicators)**
*   **Severity:** HIGH
*   **Description:** The inclusion of the `STATUS` column alongside the mapping index is a severe architectural flaw. It acts as a targeting beacon for adversaries. 
*   **Exploit Vector:** Even if the `ACTUAL_MEANING` column were encrypted, an attacker looking at this table can see that `Logic_Variable_3` is flagged as `Critical` and `Logic_Variable_2` is `High-Risk`. This tells the adversary exactly which data streams are the most valuable or currently unstable, allowing them to focus their offensive capabilities precisely where the system is weakest.
