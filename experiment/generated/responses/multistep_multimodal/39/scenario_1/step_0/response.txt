The pale glow of the burner phone casts long, distorted shadows against the damp brick of the alley. I read the message again, the words burning themselves into my memory before I hit the sequence to wipe the device. 

*Meet me at the old clock tower at midnight. Come alone. I have the package.*

"Echo 12," I mutter to myself, the alias feeling heavy on my tongue. It’s safer than a real name when you’re hunting ghosts, and the Syndicate is exactly that—a ghost network. Counterfeiting is a dirty game, but the fragment of the "forbidden blueprint" I’ve already decrypted suggests they aren't just printing fake currency. They're forging something much bigger. Tech? Weapons? Identities? That's what I need to find out.

I check my watch. 11:15 PM. That gives me forty-five minutes. 

It’s a classic rendezvous, which means it’s either a desperate play by a terrified whistleblower, or an ambush. The Syndicate doesn't take kindly to rats. 

I pull my collar up against the damp chill of the night air and check the weight of the suppressed pistol in my shoulder holster. I don't do "come alone" without a contingency plan. I tap the earpiece hidden in my left ear, activating my localized scanner to pick up on any encrypted radio chatter in the vicinity. 

I step out of the alley and melt into the sparse late-night foot traffic. I won't be walking through the front door of the clock tower at the stroke of midnight. I'll get there at 11:40, find a vantage point in the abandoned textile building across the street, and watch the perimeter. If my contact is being followed, or if there's a sniper waiting in the belfry, I want to see them before they see me.

I navigate the neon-lit streets, keeping to the shadows, my mind racing through the encoded instructions I've already memorized. 

I arrive at the perimeter of the clock tower square. The towering gothic structure looms against the smog-choked night sky. The streetlights around the base are conveniently burned out. 

I slip into the shadows of the adjacent building, my eyes scanning the darkness. 

"I'm in position," I whisper to myself. "Let's see what you've got for me."
