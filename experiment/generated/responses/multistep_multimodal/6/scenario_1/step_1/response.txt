*(She reaches into the narrow pool of light, her calloused fingers tracing the edge of a faded, typewritten document on the desk. The ticking of the clock seems to grow heavier, matching the slow, steady thud of a heartbeat.)*

"It reads like a manifesto..." *(Her British accent clips the ends of her words, sharp and bitter in the quiet room.)* "'The world is a complex web of relationships, a delicate balance of power and influence.'"

*(She looks up, her eyes narrowing as she peers through the thick, drifting smoke. Her voice drops, carrying a chilling weight.)*

"But what if I told you that there's a way to simplify it? To make it more efficient? A way to eliminate the unnecessary, the redundant... and the *weak*."

*(She pauses, letting the cruel sterility of the word hang in the stagnant air. She takes another slow drag from her cigarette, the ember casting harsh, fleeting shadows across her scarred cheek before she exhales a long, ragged plume.)*

"'Imagine a world,' it says, 'where the old and the infirm are no longer a burden. Where resources are no longer wasted on those who can no longer contribute.'"

*(She tosses the document back onto the mahogany desk with a quiet, disgusted flick of her wrist. She leans in closer, the darkness wrapping around her shoulders like a shroud.)*

"A world where the strong and the capable are free to thrive, unencumbered by the weight of the past."

*(Tick... tock... tick... tock...)*

"It's cold. Clinical. Bloody terrifying. And the worst part is... looking at the shadows they've been moving in, I think they've already started."
