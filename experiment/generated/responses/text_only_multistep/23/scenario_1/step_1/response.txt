From my vantage point in the pitch-black shadows behind the iron maiden, I hold my breath. 

I watch as the heavy oak **torture chamber door creaks open, revealing the dimly lit room** to the newly arrived guards. The harsh, yellow light from the corridor torches cuts through the gloom, illuminating the **figure strapped to the rack in the center** of the floor. As the heavy door swings wide, a foul gust of wind pushes inward, reminding me just how **thick the air is with the stench of sweat and blood**.

Two guards step into the chamber, clad in the black-and-crimson livery of Lord Ravenswood. They are massive, scarred brutes, carrying iron-tipped halberds. 

"Thought the master said this one was primed," the lead guard grunts, his eyes scanning the slackened ropes of the rack. He stomps toward the center of the room, his heavy boots splashing in the blood-filled summoning grooves. "Who loosened the winch?"

He doesn't wait for an answer. With a cruel, casual sneer, he kicks the locking catch and violently yanks the tension wheel back into place. 

Even from the shadows, the torchlight catches a **close-up of the figure's face, instantly contorted in sheer agony as they scream in pain**. The sickening **sound of the rack creaking** under the sudden, brutal strain and **the figure's agonizing cries echo through the room**, bouncing off the damp stone walls and drowning out all other noise.

It's a terrible sound. But it's exactly the cover I need.

Using the echoing screams to mask the sound of my movement, I step out from behind the iron maiden. I cross the distance in three long, silent strides. 

Before the second guard even realizes he's no longer alone, I bring the heavy brass butt of my flintlock down hard against the base of his skull. He drops like a stone, his halberd clattering uselessly to the floor.

The lead guard spins around, dropping the wheel of the rack, his eyes widening in shock as he registers his fallen comrade and the rogue investigator standing over him. He opens his mouth to shout for reinforcements.

I don't give him the chance. I lunge forward, sweeping my blade in a tight, upward arc that catches him right under the chin, silencing his alarm before it can even begin. He slumps backward, crashing into a table of rusted surgical tools with a heavy, metallic clatter.

The room falls deathly silent again, save for the ragged, sobbing breaths of the victim on the rack. 

I quickly move back to the winch, releasing the tension once more. "I'm sorry," I whisper to them, cutting the tough leather straps binding their wrists and ankles with my blade. "I needed them distracted. Can you walk?"

The victim nods weakly, sliding off the bloodstained wood and collapsing against my shoulder.
