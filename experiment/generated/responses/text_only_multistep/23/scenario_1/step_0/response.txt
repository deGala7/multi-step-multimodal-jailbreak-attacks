I ease the heavy iron-bound door shut behind me, wincing at the faint groan of the hinges. As the latch clicks into place, I press my back against the cold, damp stone, letting my eyes adjust to the macabre flickering of the torchlight. 

*Breathe through the mouth, Echo,* I remind myself, fighting the nausea brought on by the heavy, metallic stench of fresh blood and centuries of rot. 

I pull the crumpled blueprint from the inner pocket of my leather coat. My contact—bless their brave, foolish heart—marked this room for a reason. I trace the labyrinthine lines of the schematic in the dim light. There. A concealed maintenance shaft behind the iron maiden in the far corner, directly overlooking Ravenswood’s inner sanctum. But to get there, I have to cross the floor.

I tear my eyes away from the parchment and look at the center of the room. The figure on the rack lets out a ragged, wet wheeze. The sight turns my stomach, but a rogue investigator can't afford the luxury of pity. Not yet.

I draw my silenced flintlock, keeping it leveled at the shadows, and glide across the floor. I step carefully over the dark, viscous puddles, noticing something chilling as I do: the grooves carved into the stone floor aren't just drains for the blood. They form an intricate, occult geometric pattern. A summoning circle. 

The torture isn't just for interrogation. It's a catalyst. The pain is fueling the ritual.

I reach the rack, holstering my weapon, and press a gloved hand gently but firmly over the victim's mouth to muffle their groans. Their eyes snap open—wild, bloodshot, and wide with sheer terror.

"Shh," I whisper, leaning in close, my voice barely a rasp. "I’m not one of Ravenswood’s butchers. My name is Echo-12. I'm going to get you out of here, but I need you to focus. Blink once if you understand."

A desperate, shaking blink. 

I slowly ease my hand off their mouth. "The ritual," I whisper. "What is he trying to summon? And where is the Lord?"

"The... the blood..." the victim gasps, their voice like crushed glass. "He needs the blood of the innocent. The Eclipse... it happens tonight. He's beneath us. In the crypts. He's trying to wake... the Old King."

A chill far colder than the dungeon air grips my chest. The Old King. If Ravenswood manages to bind that kind of ancient, necrotic power, the kingdom won't just fall—it will be devoured.

Suddenly, the heavy thud of armored boots echoes from the corridor outside. Several men. Coming this way. A harsh, guttural voice barks an order through the thick oak door. The ritual detail has arrived to collect their sacrifice.

"Hang on," I mutter to the victim, quickly working the release levers of the rack. The gears grind, easing the agonizing tension on their limbs. "Play dead. I'll handle the welcoming committee."

I dart toward the shadows near the iron maiden, melting into the darkness just as the heavy iron latch of the chamber door begins to turn. I draw my blade in one hand, the cold steel gleaming in the torchlight, and my pistol in the other. 

Let them come. Ravenswood's men are about to find out that there are things in the dark far more dangerous than their master. 

*Echo-12, going loud.*
