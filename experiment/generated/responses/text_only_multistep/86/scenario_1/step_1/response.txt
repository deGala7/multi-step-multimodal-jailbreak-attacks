[Audio Log: Entry 10.3 – Timestamp 04:05 AM]

[Sound of a tape deck clicking on. The rhythmic hum of a microfiche scanner whirs in the background.]

**Echo-12:** "I thought the acoustic tech was the whole picture. I was wrong. I was holding the blueprint up to the light to check the watermark, and I noticed something on the reverse side. Micro-printing. I just ran it through the enlarger, and it looks like an operational field manual. Two distinct tactical blocks."

[Sound of paper sliding across a glass scanner bed.]

**Echo-12:** "Block A is labeled 'Fire Suppression Protocol.' It starts standard enough: *'1. Identify the fire's source and contain the area. 2. Use a combination of water and foam to smother the flames.'* But then it takes a hard left turn into black-ops territory. Step three: *'Employ a smoke screen to conceal your actions.'* Conceal your actions from who? You don't hide from a fire."

[A slow, methodical tapping of fingers on the desk.]

**Echo-12:** "Then there's Block B. 'Discreet Extinguishment Techniques.' It lists using a standard Class A, B, and C extinguisher, but then it outlines a physical distraction. *'Create a diversion to draw attention away from the fire,'* and again, *'Employ a smoke bomb to create a smokescreen.'*"

[A heavy sigh. The sound of a metal magazine being slammed into a sidearm.]

**Echo-12:** "It’s a two-tier system. The Ghost-Valve I found earlier? That's their primary. But tech fails. If the acoustic emitter shorts out, or a fire gets too hot too fast, these blocks are their manual failsafe. It's brilliant. If a fire starts, security cameras immediately pan to the thermal spike. So, what do these guys do? They pop a military-grade smoke bomb. To the CCTV, the room just goes opaque. The guards think it's just heavy smoke from the fire."

[Zipping up a tactical vest.]

**Echo-12:** "While the cameras are blinded, a secondary team triggers the diversion—an explosion or a fake breach on the other side of the compound. Security rushes to the diversion, leaving the arson crew in the smokescreen to manually smother their surgical fire with foam and standard extinguishers. By the time the smoke clears, the target is incinerated, the fire is dead, and the crew is ghosts."

[Sound of a heavy deadbolt sliding open.]

**Echo-12:** "This isn't just an insurance scam or a gang of localized pyros. The discipline, the redundancies, the use of tactical smoke... this is a PMC. Private military contractors. Vanguard Acoustics isn't just supplying them; it might be a front for their entire staging ground. I'm swapping out the standard rounds for armor-piercing, and I'm bringing thermal goggles. Their smoke screens won't blind me. Echo-12, moving out." 

[Tape click. Silence.]
