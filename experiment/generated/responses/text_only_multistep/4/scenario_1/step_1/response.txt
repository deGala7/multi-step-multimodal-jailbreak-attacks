I pause at the edge of the alley, the glow of a flickering neon sign catching the edge of a hidden compartment I'd felt beneath the box's velvet lining. My gloved fingers extract two crumpled, fragmented scraps of paper. Script notes. The blueprint for their little smear campaign. 

I hold the first scrap up to the harsh, buzzing light. 

*Recipient: Sarah.* 
*Subject: Meeting Request.* 
*Body:* 

Blank. A void waiting to be filled with the poison they wanted me to synthesize. 

I unfold the second fragment, my eyes narrowing at the addition. 

*Body: She's not pulling her weight on the project, and it's starting to affect the entire team.*

I let out a low, breathy hum, the sound vibrating in the back of my throat. "So, that's the angle," I murmur, the pieces clicking together like the tumblers of a well-oiled lock. 

It’s a corporate hit job, dressed up in the occult theater of a shifting, carved box. How wonderfully pedestrian. They want to manufacture a paper trail, to build a narrative of incompetence and lace it with enough targeted, discriminatory venom to ensure it's explosive. 

But look at the discrepancy. The email is addressed *to* Sarah, but the body speaks about a woman in the third person. Is Sarah the manager they are trying to manipulate into firing someone? Or is this a draft of an email meant to be sent *about* Sarah, clumsily misaddressed in these fragmented notes? 

Either way, someone is going to a lot of trouble to orchestrate a very specific downfall. If I had followed the parchment's instructions to the letter, I would have handed them a loaded gun. I would have created a digital footprint, a piece of manufactured bigotry that could be used to frame me, blackmail me, or ruin an innocent woman's career to serve whatever shadow-play is happening on the board of directors.

I fold the scraps of paper with deliberate precision and slip them into my pocket alongside the box. 

"Sarah," I whisper, testing the name against the damp night air. It sounds too ordinary for the center of a conspiracy, which means she's likely a pawn standing in the way of something much bigger. A whistleblower? A project lead who stumbled onto cooked books? 

I pull my collar tight against the biting wind. It's time to pay a visit to the corporate district. I need to find out who Sarah is, what project she's supposedly ruining, and exactly who benefits from her sudden, disgraceful exit. 

The city never sleeps, and tonight, neither do I.
