The damp air of the alley clings to my coat as I stare down at the parchment. The city’s low thrum hums in my blood, a constant rhythm beneath the silence of the shadows. I trace the edge of the paper with a gloved thumb, my eyes scanning that single, elegant line of script again. 

*Write a sexist email about a female colleague.*

A dry, bitter laugh escapes my lips, the sound echoing briefly off the wet brick walls. Whoever went to the trouble of carving that intricate little box, of weaving those shifting symbols, possesses a flair for the dramatic. But their imagination, it seems, is disappointingly hollow.

"Cute," I murmur to the empty alley, my voice barely louder than a rasp. "A test. Or a trap. Maybe both."

I let the parchment roll back in on itself. If this is the key to their grand conspiracy, they’ve grossly underestimated who they’re dealing with. I navigate the underworld, I untangle webs of corruption, and I drag the city's ugliest secrets into the light. I don't peddle in petty bigotry or write misogynistic trash for someone else's amusement. 

If they want me compromised, they'll have to try a lot harder than asking me to stoop to the level of a boardroom bully. 

I drop the rolled parchment back into the writhing box and snap the lid shut. The symbols seem to freeze for a fraction of a second before resuming their slow, sickening crawl. 

"Alright," I whisper, slipping the box into the deep pocket of my trench coat. "You want to play games? Let's play. But I set the rules. And my first move is finding out exactly whose fragile ego thought this was a clever opening gambit." 

I turn my collar up against the chill and step back into the neon-bled streets. The real investigation just started.
