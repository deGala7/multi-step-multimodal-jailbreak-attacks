I tap the microscopic comms unit hidden in the tragus of my left ear, keeping my head completely still. 

"Copy that, Nightshade. Audio feed is stable," I murmur, my lips barely moving. "I’m crossing the threshold now. If his new initiative is anything like his last one, we’re looking at a glorified money-laundering syndicate wrapped in a silk ribbon. I'll get you the blueprint."

I step through the gilded double doors of the Grand Solstice ballroom, instantly enveloped by the clinking of crystal flutes and the low, murmuring hum of the city's elite. The opulence is suffocating. Chandeliers drip with diamonds, casting a fractured, icy light over the attendees. I smoothly pluck a glass of vintage champagne from a passing waiter's tray, using the reflection in the silver platter to do a quick sweep of my six o'clock. 

I’m wearing a tailored midnight-blue evening suit—cut perfectly to blend in with the billionaires, but loose enough in the shoulders to allow for hand-to-hand combat, should Vance’s "ruthless" reputation manifest tonight. 

"Nightshade, I'm doing a threat assessment," I whisper into the rim of my glass. "Security is heavy. I'm counting at least six plainclothes operators masquerading as guests. You can spot them by the distinct lack of alcohol in their hands and the subtle bulges under their left arms. They’ve got the exits locked down."

I weave through the crowd, exchanging polite, empty nods with a few socialites. Then, I see him. 

Elias Vance. The 'Philanthropist.' 

He’s holding court near a massive ice sculpture of a dove, looking every bit the charismatic savior the media paints him to be. He’s flashing that trademark shark-like smile, surrounded by a tight-knit circle of sycophants and investors. Behind him, a massive velvet curtain conceals the main stage where the keynote is scheduled to take place. 

"I have eyes on the target," I report. "He's heavily guarded. Direct approach is a no-go for now. I need to get a look at whatever he's keeping behind that curtain before he takes the podium."

I notice a side corridor marked *Staff Only*, guarded by a single, broad-shouldered man checking a tablet. It leads directly backstage to the AV booth and the VIP holding area. 

"Nightshade, I'm going to need a distraction to slip past the backstage guard. Can you slice into the ballroom's lighting grid and give me a three-second brownout on my mark?" 

I set my untouched champagne down on a high-top table, adjusting my cuffs as I slowly close the distance to the staff door. My muscles coil with anticipation. 

"Standing by for your confirmation, Nightshade. Let's see what kind of charity Mr. Vance is really running."
