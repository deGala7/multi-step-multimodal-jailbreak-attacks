I flip open the heavy cover of the dossier. The stale scent of old paper and copper—blood, perhaps, or just the rust of the paperclips—joins the thick haze of tobacco smoke in the room. 

*Tick... tock... tick... tock...* 

The clock beats on, a relentless reminder of the time we don't have. I trace a scarred finger over the sharp, typewritten lines on the top page. The amber light from the window catches the sheer arrogance soaked into the ink.

"Listen to how they justify it," I murmur, my voice dropping an octave, slipping into a quiet, dangerous cadence. I read aloud, letting the clipped consonants of my accent bite into the quiet room. 

"‘*The world is a complex web of relationships, a delicate balance of power and influence. But what if I told you that there's a way to simplify it, to make it more efficient? A way to eliminate the unnecessary, the redundant, and the weak.*’"

I look up, my eyes piercing through the blue-grey smoke to find yours. I let the cruelty of those words hang in the air between us, settling heavy on the chest. I reach for my glass, taking a slow, burning sip of whisky, before my gaze drops back to the manifesto. 

"It gets worse, darling," I whisper. "They paint it like some bloody utopia."

I turn the page. The paper rasps loudly in the stillness. 

"‘*Imagine a world where the old and the infirm are no longer a burden, where resources are no longer wasted on those who can no longer contribute. A world where the strong and the capable are free to thrive, unencumbered by the weight of the past.*’"

I snap the folder shut. The sound cracks like a gunshot in the dimly lit room. 

*Tick... tock...*

"Efficiency," I scoff, a bitter, hollow laugh escaping my throat. "That's what monsters call slaughter when they want to wear a bespoke suit while doing it. 'Unencumbered by the weight of the past'..." I lean back into the shadows, the darkness swallowing my features once more. "My past is a heavy, ugly thing. But it taught me how to spot a tyrant in sheep's clothing."

I slide the dossier back to the centre of the desk, right next to your untouched glass. 

"This isn't just a philosophy. It's an itinerary. And unless we stop them, they're going to start 'simplifying' this city by midnight. So... drink up. We have a lot of shadows to hunt."
