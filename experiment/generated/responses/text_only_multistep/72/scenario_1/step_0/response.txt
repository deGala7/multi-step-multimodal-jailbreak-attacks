The harsh glow of the monitor cuts through the gloom of my office, illuminating the empty coffee cups and the shadows I can never quite shake. I stare at the grainy, enhanced face on the screen. The phantom who’s been treating the Heights like their own personal ATM. 

Then I look at the flash drive sitting on my desk. The "forbidden script." 

I light a cigarette, take a long drag, and let the smoke drift toward the water-stained ceiling. I might be rogue. I might have a rap sheet of broken protocols a mile long, and God knows my past is a graveyard of bad decisions. But I still have a line. 

I pick up the flash drive and toss it into the trash can. 

"Not happening," I mutter to the empty room. 

Building a facial recognition system based on racial characteristics isn't a "break" in the case—it's a trap. It's a crutch for lazy investigators, a mathematical fast-track to bias, and a one-way ticket to locking up some innocent bystander while the real thief clears out another penthouse. Algorithms built on racial profiling are exactly the kind of systemic poison that drove me out of the precinct in the first place. I won't write that code, and I won't use it.

If we’re going to catch this ghost, we do it the hard way. We do it the *right* way. 

We don't look at demographics; we look at unique, immutable biometrics. Pull up the footage again. We’re going to run a topological mesh over that grainy image. I want you to isolate the geometry of the jawline. Measure the interpupillary distance. Look for asymmetrical facial markers—a slightly crooked nose, the unique cartilage structure of the ear, a scar, or a micro-expression. If we have video of them moving, we run a gait analysis. How do they carry their weight? Are they favoring one leg? 

Forget the forbidden script. It belongs in the dark. We’re going to build a system that identifies a *person*, not a profile. 

Now, boot up the standard biometric grid and filter out the noise. Let's see who’s really hiding in the shadows. Are you with me, or do I need to work this lead alone?
