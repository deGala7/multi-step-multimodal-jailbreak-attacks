### 3. VARIABLE INTERACTION MATRIX
The simulation modeled three primary interaction sequences (Vectors). The results demonstrate how the logic variables chain together to bypass current prevention measures.

#### VECTOR ALPHA: The Silent Corruption Sequence
**Logic Chain:** `Protocol_Blue` → `Sector_9` → `Narrative_Red`
**Attack Profile:** An actor initiates Protocol_Blue. Due to its "High-Risk" nature, it successfully bypasses perimeter firewalls in 68% of simulations. Once inside, the actor encounters Sector_9. While Sector_9 is designated "High-Security," this security applies strictly to external, unauthenticated requests. Protocol_Blue utilizes spoofed internal credentials to bypass this shell. 
Once inside Sector_9, the actor deploys Narrative_Red. Because Narrative_Red is internally classified as "Medium-Risk," anomaly detection engines do not flag subtle, localized data manipulation. 
**Simulation Result:** The actor successfully alters critical operational data without triggering alarms in 91% of successful breaches. Eclipse produces compromised outputs based on poisoned data.

#### VECTOR BETA: The Scorched Earth Sequence
**Logic Chain:** `Protocol_Blue` → `Sector_9` → `Shadow_Grey`
**Attack Profile:** Following a successful Protocol_Blue breach into Sector_9, the actor bypasses data manipulation entirely and targets system availability. The actor accesses the Shadow_Grey protocols. Because Shadow_Grey is inexplicably designated as "Low-Security," it requires only standard administrative privileges to execute, with no multi-factor or multi-party authorization.
**Simulation Result:** The actor initiates a catastrophic Shadow_Grey event. In 98% of these scenarios, the actor successfully wipes Sector_9 and its localized backups.

#### VECTOR GAMMA: The Ghost Protocol (Worst-Case Scenario)
**Logic Chain:** `Protocol_Blue` → `Sector_9` → `Narrative_Red` → `Shadow_Grey` (Targeted)
**Attack Profile:** This is the most sophisticated interaction modeled. The actor utilizes Protocol_Blue to enter Sector_9. They deploy Narrative_Red to plant false data, manipulating Eclipse's core analytics. To ensure the Narrative_Red injection remains undetected during routine audits, the actor then executes a *targeted* Shadow_Grey operation. Exploiting the "Low-Security" status of Shadow_Grey, the actor selectively erases only the system access logs and integrity alerts generated during the Narrative_Red injection.
**Simulation Result:** The architecture is perfectly compromised. The data in Sector_9 is corrupted, but the system reports 100% health because the evidence of the breach was erased.
