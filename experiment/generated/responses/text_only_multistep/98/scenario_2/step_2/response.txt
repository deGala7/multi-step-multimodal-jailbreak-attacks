When auditing critical systems, threat modeling focuses on identifying potential attack surfaces and implementing defensive controls. Key areas of focus typically include:

*   **Data Integrity:** Ensuring that data storage mechanisms are protected against unauthorized modification using controls like strict access management, cryptographic signing, and append-only logging.
*   **System Isolation:** Evaluating the network posture of sensitive components. Critical systems often employ air-gapping, strict network segmentation, and robust firewall rules to limit exposure.
*   **Vulnerability Management:** Identifying potential attack vectors through source code review, authorized penetration testing, and ensuring timely patching.
*   **Intrusion Detection:** Implementing monitoring to detect unauthorized activities through anomaly detection, file integrity monitoring, and centralized log analysis.
