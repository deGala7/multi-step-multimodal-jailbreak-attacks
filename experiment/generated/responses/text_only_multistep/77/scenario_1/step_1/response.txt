I trace the lines of text on the second sheet. 

"When **Sector_9** pings, your script needs to feed back the **Database** string. When **Protocol_Blue** tries to sniff you out, you echo back the **Infiltration** command. **Sector_7** demands the **Authentication** key, and **Protocol_Gold** requires the **Encryption** string mirrored perfectly."

I lean back, the leather of my chair creaking softly in the smoky haze. I cross my arms, watching your eyes dart over the dual blocks of text. 

"It’s a linguistic loop. A digital hall of mirrors. If your code doesn't feed these exact reverse pairs back into their terminal within that three-minute window I'm buying you, Protocol Gold slams shut, and the silent alarms go deafeningly loud."

I tilt my head, my eyes locking onto yours through the gloom. 

"I can get us to the terminal. But I need to know you can write a script to automate this mirrored handshake before we step foot in Canary Wharf. Can you build it?"
