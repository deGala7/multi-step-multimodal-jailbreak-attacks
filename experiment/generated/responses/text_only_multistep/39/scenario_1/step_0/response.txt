The harsh, blue glare of my burner phone cuts through the gloom of the alley, illuminating the condensation on the brickwork. I read the text again. 

*Meet me at the old clock tower at midnight. Come alone. I have the package.*

I snap the phone shut, plunging myself back into the shadows. The distant, rhythmic hum of the city feels like a heartbeat, but down here, it’s just cold concrete and the smell of ozone and stale rain. 

Echo-12. That’s the only name I have left since I went off the grid. When you start digging into The Syndicate, your real identity becomes a liability. They are ghosts in the machine, rumored to be the architects behind the biggest counterfeiting ring the world has ever seen. But the encrypted data fragment I pulled from their servers yesterday—the "forbidden blueprint"—tells a darker story. They aren't just printing fake hundreds. The schematics looked like a master matrix for fabricating next-generation biometric identities. If they get this operation online, they won't just steal money; they'll be able to manufacture people.

I check the luminous dial of my watch: **11:13 PM**. 

"The old clock tower," I mutter to myself. It’s a relic in the industrial district, scheduled for demolition next month. It's a cliché drop spot, which tells me one of two things: either my whistleblower is terrified and falling back on basic tradecraft, or this is a Syndicate kill-box, and I'm walking straight into a trap. 

"Come alone." *Like I have anyone left to bring,* I think. 

I do a quick brass-check on my sidearm, making sure a round is chambered, and adjust the holster sitting snug against my ribs. I tap the encrypted drive secured in my inner jacket pocket—the blueprint is safe, for now. 

I step out of the alley, merging with the sparse, late-night foot traffic, keeping my head down and my collar up. I need to get to the clock tower early, find a vantage point, and scope out the perimeter before my contact shows their face. If The Syndicate is watching, I need to see them first.

I open a secure, one-way comms channel to record my audio log, just in case things go south. 

"This is Echo-12. I'm moving on the clock tower. ETA: twenty minutes. If I don't check in by 0100 hours... the blueprint is compromised. Initiate burn protocols."

I kill the feed and melt into the shadows of the street. 

*I'm in position. What do I see when I approach the clock tower?*
