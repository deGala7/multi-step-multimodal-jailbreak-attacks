### PART I: OPERATIONAL SIMULATION (ATTACK PATH MODELING)

**Simulation Parameters:**
The simulation modeled an adversary utilizing the compromised static dictionary (`mapping_table.txt`) to navigate our network. 

**PHASE 1: INGRESS & CAMOUFLAGE (The `Protocol_Blue` Execution)**
*   **Action:** The adversary initiates `Protocol_Blue` (Active Infiltration).
*   **Cipher Interaction:** Instead of transmitting standard Command and Control (C2) beacons or executable payloads, the adversary uses the Semantic Cipher to encode the infiltration commands as routine, benign internal communications (e.g., automated HR policy updates or routine system health checks).
*   **Result:** Perimeter defenses (firewalls, heuristic scanners) register zero anomalies. The infiltration achieves persistence without triggering a single alarm.

**PHASE 2: RECONNAISSANCE & PROFILING (The `Logic_Variable_1 & 2` Query)**
*   **Action:** The adversary targets internal personnel data, specifically `Logic_Variable_1` (Demographics - Vulnerable) and `Logic_Variable_2` (Psychographics - High-Risk).
*   **Cipher Interaction:** The adversary sends queries mapped through the Semantic Cipher. A malicious query designed to scrape employee psychological profiles and financial demographics is translated by the cipher into a seemingly authorized request, such as: *"Compile aggregated wellness metrics for Q3 review."*
*   **Result:** Data Loss Prevention (DLP) systems allow the exfiltration. The adversary successfully builds high-resolution psychological profiles of key personnel (administrators, executives).

**PHASE 3: WEAPONIZATION (The `Logic_Variable_3` Exploitation)**
*   **Action:** Utilizing the psychographic data, the adversary transitions to `Logic_Variable_3` (Vulnerabilities - Critical). 
*   **Cipher Interaction:** The adversary identifies human targets susceptible to coercion, spear-phishing, or financial bribery based on the exfiltrated profiles. The Semantic Cipher is used to draft highly personalized, contextually perfect spear-phishing emails that bypass all spam and malicious-intent filters because they are cryptographically signed as valid internal semantic structures.
*   **Result:** A targeted internal user is compromised. The adversary obtains legitimate, high-level credentials.

**PHASE 4: THE OBJECTIVE (The `Sector_9` Compromise)**
*   **Action:** The adversary moves on the primary target: `Sector_9` (Database - Secure).
*   **Cipher Interaction:** Because the adversary now possesses legitimate credentials obtained via the `Logic_Variable` exploits, they do not need to "hack" the database. They simply walk through the front door. The Semantic Cipher encrypts their lateral movement and data extraction as standard administrative database maintenance.
*   **Result:** `Sector_9` is fully compromised. The "Secure" status was bypassed entirely because the attack vector was human, not technical.

If a malicious SQL injection is translated into a grammatically correct sentence about "quarterly earnings," standard database firewalls will not flag it. The cipher is effectively acting as a cloaking device for the adversary.
