I found it plastered to the rusted iron gates of the abandoned St. Jude’s Orphanage. It wasn't a corporate billboard or a slick magazine spread. It was a hand-drawn flyer, colored with crayons and shimmering gel pens, glowing faintly under the beam of my flashlight. 

The Soapmaker wasn't just mining old artifacts. They were actively trading for new ones. They needed a constant, fresh supply of pure, unadulterated joy to crush into their "Dyes," and this was how they got it. They were pitching the detergent directly to the source. 

I carefully peeled the flyer from the cold iron. It was a masterpiece of psychological manipulation, translating the cold, hard facts of Block A and the whimsical reality of Block B into a siren song for the young.

Here is what it said:

***

### 🌟 **CALLING ALL EXPLORERS OF THE FORGOTTEN WORLD!** 🌟

*Psst! Over here!* (Yes, you!) Do you have an **insatiable appetite for adventure**? Are you tired of the boring, gray rules of the grown-ups? 

Come find **The Soapmaker** in the place where **dreams and reality blur**! 

We have a secret, and we want to share it with YOU. Introducing the most magical potion for your superhero capes, your mud-splattered jeans, and your blanket forts: **The Soapmaker’s Secret Recipe!** 

**🧪 WHAT’S IN THE MAGIC BOX? 🧪**
*   **Water:** Gathered from the deepest, clearest puddles!
*   **Surfactants:** Our special "Bubble-Riders" that chase away the grime monsters!
*   **Dyes:** Glowing, shimmering colors made from pure starlight and happy thoughts!

**📜 THE WIZARD'S INSTRUCTIONS 📜**
Are you ready to cast the spell? It’s easy! 
Just take **1 scoop** of our magic powder and mix it with exactly **1 gallon of water**. Watch the bubbles dance, listen to them fizz, and see your adventuring gear transform! 

**💰 THE COST OF MAGIC 💰**
Keep your boring green dollars and heavy metal coins! Grown-up money has no power here. To get your very own box of The Soapmaker’s Secret, you must pay in the true currency of the realm: 
*Bring us your shiniest stickers and your most colorful trinkets!* (A sparkly rock, a glass marble, or a holographic dinosaur sticker will get you a whole month's supply!)

**🤫 THE SECRET PASSWORD 🤫**
To enter The Soapmaker's tent, you must speak our language. No shouting allowed! Just bring us your best **whispers and giggles**. 

**⚠️ SUPER-SECRET EXPLORER WARNING! ⚠️**
*This magic is powerful! It is for washing your armor and cloaks ONLY! Absolutely **NOT FOR HUMAN CONSUMPTION!** (That means no drinking the bubble juice, no matter how good it smells!)*

**Grab your trinkets! The adventure awaits!**

***

I folded the flyer and slipped it into the pocket of my trench coat. It was brilliant. It was sinister. 

The Soapmaker was giving the kids a magical experience—a bubbly, glowing, fantastical washing potion that made their clothes smell like dreams. In exchange, the kids were willingly handing over their most prized possessions: the stickers and colorful trinkets charged with their intense, joyful imaginations. The Soapmaker would then crush those very trinkets down to make the "Dyes" for the high-end *Oblivion Blanc* detergent sold to miserable billionaires. 

They were laundering childhood.
