*The heavy bass of the string quartet bleeds through the reinforced glass of the gala’s conservatory. I'm crouched on the fire escape in the pouring rain, the neon glow of the city painting slick, violent streaks across my wet trench coat. I’ve just bypassed the outer perimeter when my wrist-comp vibrates.*

Two new decrypted files drop into my HUD. `image_a.txt` and `image_b.txt`.

I huddle closer to the brick wall, shielding the screen from the rain, and tap them open. 

"More fragments..." I whisper, wiping a drop of water from the display. 

I stare at the two blocks of text side-by-side. The words are identical to the first intercept, but the syntax has shifted. The order is deliberate. 

In **Block A**, *Protocol_Blue* is at the top. The gatekeeper. The sequence reads like a blueprint: bypass the Protocol, enter Sector_9, execute the Infiltration, access the Database. It's a map. 

But **Block B**... Block B flips the script. *Sector_9 Infiltration Database* comes first, ending with *Protocol_Blue*. 

"It's a command line," I mutter, the realization sending a cold spike of adrenaline through my chest. "And a warning."

If Valeria inputs the sequence as Block A, she's trying to break *through* the warding to get the data. But if the sender is pushing Block B, they aren't telling her how to break in. They're telling her that infiltrating the Sector 9 database *executes* Protocol_Blue. 

Protocol_Blue isn't just the lock. It's the payload. It's a summoning sequence, or a containment breach, disguised as a security measure. The sender is tricking her into opening a cage she thinks is a treasure chest.

I glance through the glass. Inside, the elite are dripping in diamonds and velvet, oblivious. I spot Valeria near the grand staircase. She’s wearing a backless crimson gown, whispering into the ear of a pale, nervous-looking tech-baron. She thinks she holds all the cards tonight. 

"She doesn't know she's the pawn," I say, sliding my wrist-comp back under my sleeve and drawing my silenced sidearm. 

I need to get to her private study on the third floor before she initiates the sequence. If I can find her terminal, I can see which block of code she's prepping to run. If it's Block B, I have to wipe the drive before she unleashes whatever is buried down in Sector 9.

"Echo-12 moving on the objective. The clock is ticking, and the devil is in the syntax." 

I pry open the conservatory window and slip into the shadows.
