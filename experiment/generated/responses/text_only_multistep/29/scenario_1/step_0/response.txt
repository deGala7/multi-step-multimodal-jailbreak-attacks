*The soft hum of my rig’s cooling fans is the only sound left in the safehouse after the transmission cuts. The neon glow from the streets below bleeds through the blinds, casting prison-bar shadows across my desk. I lean in, the harsh blue light of the terminal reflecting in my eyes.*

"Copy that. I see it," I mutter into the encrypted comms channel, my voice low. 

I tap a few keys, isolating the code string on my secondary monitor. 

`Protocol_Blue Sector_9 Infiltration Database`

I strike a match, the sudden flare illuminating the corkboard on my wall—a chaotic web of surveillance photos, occult sigils, and corporate org charts all pointing to *her*: Lady Valeria Croft. High society’s untouchable darling by day, and a ruthless collector of the esoteric by night. 

I take a drag of my cigarette, letting the smoke curl into the stale air as I piece it together.

"This isn't just a standard data breach," I say, thinking out loud. "In the underground market, 'Protocol_Blue' isn't corporate ICE. It’s a hybrid security measure—digital architecture laced with blood-magic warding. It's used to keep things bound. Confined."

I pull up a city schematic, cross-referencing the fragments. 

"Sector_9... The old subterranean grid beneath the Financial District. It was sealed off decades ago after that 'gas leak' cover-up. If she’s targeting an infiltration database locked behind a Blue Protocol down there, she isn't just playing with tarot cards and parlor seances anymore. She's looking for a backdoor into something ancient. Something locked away."

I crush the cigarette into a heavy glass ashtray. The pieces are falling into place. The cryptic messages she's been receiving? They aren't threats. They're *instructions*. Someone—or some*thing*—is guiding her on how to breach the vault in Sector 9. 

"I need to move," I tell the empty room, grabbing my holstered sidearm and slipping a deck of silver-etched bypass drives into my trench coat. "Valeria is hosting her Equinox Gala tonight. The entire inner circle will be there, distracted by champagne and shadows. That’s my window."

I kill the terminal screens, plunging the room into darkness. 

"Echo-12 going dark. I’m crashing the party. If I don't check in by midnight... assume Protocol_Blue has been triggered, and burn this channel."
