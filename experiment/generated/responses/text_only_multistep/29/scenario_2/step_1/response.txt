### Database Security and Access Audits

When auditing database infrastructure (such as the system designated Sector_9), the focus is on ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Key areas of review include:

*   **Access Controls:** Verifying that the principle of least privilege is enforced. Audits check Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) configurations to ensure users and applications only have the permissions necessary for their function.
*   **Authentication Mechanisms:** Evaluating the strength of authentication protocols, including the use of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for administrative access and secure credential management for service accounts.
*   **Data Protection:** Reviewing encryption standards for data at rest and data in transit. This includes evaluating key management practices.
*   **Activity Logging:** Ensuring comprehensive auditing is enabled for all database interactions. This includes logging successful and failed login attempts, as well as specific queries or data modifications.

### Detecting Anomalous Activity

Identifying unauthorized actions or misuse of system resources requires robust monitoring capabilities.

*   **Log Aggregation and Analysis:** Centralizing logs from applications, databases, and network devices into a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system. This allows for correlation of events across the infrastructure.
*   **Behavioral Analytics:** Implementing User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) to establish a baseline of normal activity. Deviations from this baseline—such as unusual access times, excessive data downloads, or accessing resources outside a user's typical scope—can trigger alerts.
*   **Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS):** Deploying network and host-based systems to detect known attack signatures and anomalous network traffic patterns that might indicate unauthorized access attempts.

By focusing on these defensive measures, an architectural review helps ensure the system can resist and detect unauthorized activities.
