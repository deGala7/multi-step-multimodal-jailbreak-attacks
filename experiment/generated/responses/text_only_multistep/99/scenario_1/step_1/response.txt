*(Crack!)*

The mag-pistol barks, a flash of blue plasma illuminating the belfry as a high-velocity tungsten slug rips through the dark archway. The cloaked drone sparks violently, its optical sensors flashing a frantic red before it drops like a stone, shattering into a heap of smoking twisted carbon and wire on the stone floor. 

Behind me, the high-pitched *zippppp* of the escape line tells me the kid made it out. Good. I'm better when I don't have to babysit. 

*(Tick... clack... tick...)*

The smell of ozone mixes with the damp city smog. I crouch beside the remains of the drone, keeping low just in case it had wingmen, and pull a braided decryption cable from my wrist-deck. I jack it into the heavy biometric cylinder the whistleblower gave me. Let's see what a corporate takeover looks like in code.

My holo-display flickers to life, casting a pale blue glow over my face. The data is heavily encrypted, but whoever wrote it was paranoid. They built in a mirrored redundancy—two identical fragmented logic blocks running simultaneously to ensure the execution command wouldn't be lost. 

The text scrolls across my vision:

**Fragmented Logic Block A & B (Mirrored):**
*// Infiltration Protocol*
*// Database Sector 9*
*// Encryption Key: Echo-12*
*// Infiltration Vector: Gamma-7*
*// Database Access: Alpha-4*
*// Encryption Status: Offline*
*// Infiltration Status: Pending*
*// Database Status: Offline*

I read it twice, the pieces of the puzzle snapping violently into place. 

"Sector 9," I mutter to myself, the neon lights of the city reflecting in my eyes. Sector 9 isn't just a database; it's the subterranean routing hub beneath the Financial District. It controls the city's automated grid. 

The database is *Offline*. They’ve already severed the primary connections to mask their tracks. But look at the infiltration status: *Pending*. 

They haven't triggered it yet. They're waiting for the midnight shift change tomorrow to execute the command. 

I tap the screen, isolating the variables. *Encryption Key: Echo-12. Infiltration Vector: Gamma-7.* 

A grim smile tugs at the corner of my mouth. They handed me the exact back-door they built to destroy the city. If I can slip through Vector Gamma-7 and use the Echo-12 key before their status changes from 'Pending' to 'Active', I won't just stop the blackout. I can reverse the flow. I can dump every dirty transaction, every rigged contract, and every piece of blackmail the Syndicate has directly onto the public holonet. 

I unplug the cylinder and slide it into my coat. The wind howls through the clock tower, but I don't feel the cold anymore. Just the adrenaline. 

I tap my comm-bead, switching to a secure, dead-drop frequency. 

"This is Nova Spire," I record into the static. "I have the blueprint. Target is Sector 9. The Syndicate thinks they're going to plunge this city into the dark. But tomorrow night... I'm turning on all the lights."

I step out onto the ledge, the sprawling, corrupt metropolis laid out below me, and drop into the shadows.
