The static in my earpiece dies, replaced by the rhythmic drumming of the Macau rain against my leather collar. I tap the comms unit hidden behind my ear, sending a double-click of acknowledgment. No words. Voice signatures can be tracked, and the syndicate—the Golden Triad—has ears everywhere in this city. 

I check my chronometer. 11:14 PM. That gives me exactly forty-six minutes to navigate the labyrinth of Macau’s underbelly and reach the rendezvous. 

"Come alone." It’s the oldest trap in the book, but if this contact actually has the schematics to the *Nova Fortuna* casino's rigged machines, it’s a trap I have to walk into. Those slots aren't just skimming off the top; they're operating on a predictive algorithm that borders on quantum computing. It’s bleeding the city dry, funneling billions into a dark-money pipeline I intend to blow wide open.

I step out of the noodle shop, letting the neon lights—bleeding wounds of magenta, cyan, and gold—wash over me. The Cotai Strip looms in the distance, a fortress of glass and greed, but my path takes me the opposite way, toward the older, forgotten parts of the city where the Portuguese colonial architecture rots beneath the glow of holographic billboards. 

As I walk, I do a mental inventory of my gear. EMP emitter disguised as a Zippo lighter in my left pocket. A sleek, monomolecular blade sheathed at my forearm. And my custom datapad, loaded with enough brute-force decryption software to fry a small bank's mainframe, resting against my ribs. 

I stick to the alleys, moving through the steam rising from street-food carts and the shadows cast by hanging laundry. I keep my head down, letting the brim of my hat shield my face from the ubiquitous facial-recognition cameras perched like gargoyles on the streetlamps. 

By 11:52 PM, I arrive at the square. 

The old clock tower stands like a decaying tooth amidst the encroaching hyper-modern skyline. The cobblestones are slick with rain and devoid of tourists. It’s quiet. Too quiet. 

I melt into the shadows of a nearby archway, activating the thermal overlay in my left contact lens. I sweep the rooftops. *Clear.* I sweep the alleyways. *Clear.* Just a couple of stray cats fighting over scraps. 

At exactly 11:59, a shadow detaches itself from the base of the tower. 

I don't draw my weapon, but my hand hovers inches from it. I step out of the archway, my boots making soft, deliberate sounds on the wet stone. 

"Time is money, Echo," a voice whispers from the darkness, synthesized and distorted. 

"And the house always wins," I reply, giving the agreed-upon countersign. "Unless you know how to read the cards. You said you had the blueprint."

The figure steps forward just enough for the ambient neon to catch the edge of a metallic briefcase chained to their wrist. 

"I have it," the contact says, their voice trembling slightly. "But the syndicate... they know there's a leak. If you plug this into the *Nova Fortuna's* mainframe, you'll have a three-minute window before their ICE fries your brain and sends a hit squad to your exact coordinates. You still want it?"

I hold out my hand, my eyes locked on the shadowy figure. 

"Hand it over," I say, my voice dead calm. "I didn't come to Macau for the egg tarts."
