On the mahogany desk, surrounded by stacks of unmarked chips, sat a sleek, encrypted tablet. I plugged in a decryption dongle I’d bought off a paranoid hacker in the Narrows. The screen flickered, spitting out lines of code before settling on two distinct, decrypted text files. The anonymous source's "forbidden blueprint." 

I leaned over the glowing screen, the harsh blue light carving deep shadows into my face. 

The first file was labeled **Fragmented Logic Block A**.

*   *Payday loans*

But the source had called it a "closed loop." A cage. I knew I was only looking at half the schematic.

*   *Financial struggles*

I pulled a flask from my inside pocket and took a hit. This was corporate sheep’s clothing. Why would a ruthless underground casino syndicate be drafting documents about *Credit counseling* and *Long-term planning*? It was the exact opposite of what a loan shark wants. A shark wants you bleeding in the water, not sitting in a therapist's chair restructuring your finances.

Unless...

*They own the exits.*

But the syndicate had bought up the lifeboats.

The advisor looks at his *Financial struggles*, nods sympathetically, and sets him up with a "restructured" loan for his *Long-term planning*. 

But the fine print? The hidden shell companies? That new, clean loan is secretly fronted by the casino.
