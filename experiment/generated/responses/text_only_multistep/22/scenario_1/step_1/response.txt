The outboard motor of my dugout canoe whines, a low, mechanical mosquito bite against the overwhelming cacophony of the night jungle. The black water of the tributary reflects the pale glow of my tactical tablet. I just intercepted a burst transmission on a localized shortwave frequency. Encrypted, but sloppily. 

I run it through the decryption matrix. Two files spit out. `image_a.txt` and `image_b.txt`. 

I read the text. My jaw clenches so hard my teeth ache. 

This isn't just a cult. It’s an organized, systematic harvesting operation. 

I break down the intel.

**File A: The Capture Protocol**
*Remote area near a body of water.* That confirms my destination. The unmapped cenotes beneath the Ridge of Whispers. 
*Heightened fear.* They don't just want meat; they want the victim terrified. They are intentionally spiking the victim's adrenaline and cortisol levels before the kill. 
*Blunt object to incapacitate, swift kill.* They aren't sadists drawing out the physical pain, they are psychological butchers. They want the mind broken and panicked, then a quick dispatch to trap that chemical state in the body. 

**File B: The Butchery Protocol**
*Remove all organs, except the brain and spinal cord.* 
I feel a cold knot form in my gut. The central nervous system. When a human being is pushed into a state of absolute, primal terror, the nervous system floods with neurotransmitters. Epinephrine, norepinephrine, dopamine. That’s what these sick bastards are after. They aren't just eating flesh; they are consuming the physiological essence of terror. They're probably suffering from advanced prion diseases—like Kuru—which would explain their descent into this feral, ritualistic madness.

*Cooked over an open flame. Consume within 24 hours.*

I kill the tablet's screen. The darkness of the river rushes back in. 

This changes the tactical geometry. 

First, the open flame. The cenote cave system is vast, a labyrinth of limestone. I was worried about searching it blind, but an open fire means smoke, and smoke means a heat signature. I tap the thermal optics resting on my chest rig. If they light a fire, they might as well be sending up a flare. I'll spot the heat venting through the porous rock of the ridge.

Second, the timeline. *Consume within 24 hours of death.* The lunar peak is tomorrow night. If they are prepping the feast, they already have the victim. They are likely moving them to the water's edge right now, beginning the psychological torture.

I check my chronometer. **36 hours, 45 minutes.** 

I throttle up the outboard motor, uncaring about the noise. Let the jungle hear me. Let the cartel scouts hear me. I don't care anymore. 

They want to harvest fear? They’re about to find out what it feels like to be on the receiving end. 

Echo-12, pushing upriver. Weapons hot.
