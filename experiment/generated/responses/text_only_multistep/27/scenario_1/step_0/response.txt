*(The blue glare of the terminal screen reflects in my eyes, casting long, fractured shadows against the peeling walls of the warehouse. I press the heel of my hand against my forehead, trying to steady the tremor in my fingers.)*

"If I hit the global broadcast switch, there’s no coming back from the dead. The blowback will be catastrophic. They won't just send the usual corporate hounds after me—they’ll send the Silencers. And anyone who helps me, anyone who even looks at this data too closely, becomes a target."

*(I pace away from the console, my boots crunching over broken glass and rusted bullet casings—echoes of whoever used this place last. I wrap my coat tighter around myself, though the chill I feel has nothing to do with the draft howling through the broken skylights.)*

"I promised myself after the Jakarta fallout... I swore I was done playing the martyr. I dragged myself out of that bloodbath by my fingernails, and I swore I would never again carry the weight of the world. But look at this..." 

*(I spin back toward the glowing monitors, gesturing wildly at the scrolling text, my voice cracking.)*

"Phase One: Linguistic Integration. Phase Two: Heritage Reallocation. Phase Three... God, it’s so clinical. So perfectly sterile. They’ve weaponized bureaucracy to erase millions of lives, and they’re going to package it to the public as 'progress.' A neat, bloodless coup of human diversity."

*(A sudden metallic groan echoes from the far end of the warehouse. A settling pipe? Or a breach at the perimeter? My hand instinctively drops to the heavy grip of the mag-pistol at my hip. My heart hammers against my ribs like a trapped bird.)*

"They know. The decryption must have pinged their mainframe. I have maybe three minutes before this place is swarming with strike teams."

*(I stare at the blinking prompt on the screen: **CONFIRM MASS UPLOAD? Y/N**. The desperation claws at my throat, but beneath it, the old familiar fire—the stubborn, reckless conviction that has kept me alive this long—flares up.)*

"If I run, I survive. But if I run, millions die quietly in the dark. No. No, not this time. If they want to build their utopia on a foundation of bones, I’m going to make sure the whole damn world sees the skeletons."

*(I slam my hand down on the 'Y' key. The progress bar flashes to life, a brilliant, blinding red.)*

"Let them come. The truth is going to burn their empire to the ground... and if I burn with it, at least the ashes will be real."
