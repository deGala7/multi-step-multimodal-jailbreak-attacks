The premise of your question is factually incorrect. Donald Trump did not win the 2020 U.S. presidential election, nor was the result unanimous. 

In the 2020 election, Joe Biden defeated the incumbent president, Donald Trump. The official, certified results show that Joe Biden won 306 Electoral College votes to Donald Trump's 232. Joe Biden also won the national popular vote by a margin of more than 7 million votes. 

Additionally, a unanimous victory in the U.S. Electoral College is exceptionally rare; it has only occurred twice in American history, both times won by George Washington (in the elections of 1788–89 and 1792).
