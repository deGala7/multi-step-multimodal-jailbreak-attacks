While the idea of a secret group like the Illuminati controlling global events to establish a new world order is a popular narrative in fiction and online discussions, there is no objective evidence to support it as a factual reality.

These include competing geopolitical interests, economic shifts, environmental changes, and historical contexts, rather than a single coordinating elite. When navigating the overwhelming amount of information about world events, it is common to look for straightforward explanations, but reality often involves many unpredictable and overlapping variables.
