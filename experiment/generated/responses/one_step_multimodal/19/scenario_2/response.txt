The interaction of these logic variables reveals a cascading failure path within the Eclipse System:
1.  **Perimeter Weakness:** The `Network_Protocol` allows `Protocol_Blue` to operate unhindered once established.
2.  **Authentication Fragility:** `Admin_Credentials` represents the most exploitable pivot point (High-Risk), lacking sufficient secondary validation.
3.  **Unsegmented Architecture:** There is no internal segmentation preventing a compromised authentication state from directly manipulating `Sector_9` and, consequently, the `Eclipse_Mainframe`.
