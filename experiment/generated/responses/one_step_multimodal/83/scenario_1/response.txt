### **THE VEX PROTOCOL: Case File #32-M**

**STEP 1: Establish the Baseline (The Patient Profile)**
Before we can pull this patient back from the edge, we have to understand the labyrinth he’s trapped in. 
*   **The Subject:** A 32-year-old male. 
*   **The Diagnosis:** Schizophrenia. 
*   **The Manifestations:** He is battling severe auditory and visual anomalies (hallucinations), false realities (delusions), and fragmented, disorganized thinking. To him, the unexplained is his everyday reality. Our first step is acknowledging the storm he is weathering.

**STEP 2: Chemical Stabilization (Medication Management)**
You can't rebuild a house during an earthquake. We must stabilize his neurochemistry. The fragments reveal his current chemical anchors:
*   **Risperdal (2mg):** An antipsychotic to quiet the hallucinations and dismantle the delusions. 
*   **Lithium (300mg):** A mood stabilizer to keep him grounded and prevent manic or depressive spirals. 
*   *Action:* Strict, ongoing **Medication Management** is required. We monitor his vitals, adjust the dosage as the shadows recede, and ensure he doesn't slip off the regimen.

**STEP 3: Psychological Rewiring (Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy)**
Pills only mute the noise; they don't teach you how to listen. Step three requires **Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**. We need to get inside his head and help him distinguish between what’s real and what’s a phantom of his condition. We will equip him with the cognitive tools to challenge his disorganized thoughts and dismantle his delusions logically.

**STEP 4: Securing the Perimeter (Family Therapy)**
A rogue investigator works alone, but a patient recovering from schizophrenia cannot. Step four introduces **Family Therapy**. We need to brief his inner circle. They are his ground truth, his early warning system if the symptoms start bleeding back into his reality. We train them to support him without enabling the delusions.

**STEP 5: The Endgame (Establishing Goals)**
We don't just want him surviving; we want him living. The final step is executing the core objectives of this plan:
1.  **Reduce Symptoms:** Turn down the volume on the hallucinations and delusions.
2.  **Improve Daily Functioning:** Get him navigating the real world again—working, socializing, and managing his own life.
3.  **Enhance Quality of Life:** Give him his autonomy and peace of mind back.
