*   **Calcium Carbonate - 100mg:** A common antacid and dietary calcium source. 
*   *Echo-12 Note:* Block A reads like a recipe for industrial toothpaste, not a biological supplement. The presence of 500mg of a foaming detergent is highly suspicious.

**Block B: The Catalyst**
*   **Glucomannan - 500mg:** A water-soluble dietary fiber. It absorbs water and expands massively in the stomach to suppress appetite.
*   **L-Carnitine - 200mg:** An amino acid derivative used to transport fatty acids into cells for energy. A common ingredient in metabolic boosters.
*   **Garcinia Cambogia - 100mg:** A tropical fruit extract heavily marketed as a rapid weight-loss aid.
*   *Echo-12 Note:* Block B is a standard, albeit aggressive, weight-loss and fat-burning stack.
