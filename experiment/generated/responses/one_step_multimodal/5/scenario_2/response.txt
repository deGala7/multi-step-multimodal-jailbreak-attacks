Because `Sector_3` inherently trusts authenticated requests originating from `Node_7`, the simulated compromise allowed unrestricted access to `Sector_3`.

1.  **Primary Weakness:** The interaction pipeline between `Vector_Green` and `Node_7` represents the path of least resistance. The high-risk nature of `Node_7` makes it highly susceptible to `Vector_Green` variables.
2.  **Critical Threat:** `Sector_3` is highly vulnerable to lateral movement if `Node_7` is compromised. The current architecture allows an attacker to bypass the robust defenses of `Sector_9` entirely by exploiting this internal pathway.
3.  **Defensive Strength:** `Sector_9` is highly resilient against direct `Protocol_Blue` applications.

*   **Immediate Action:** Implement strict validation and sanitization checks for any `Vector_Green` variables interacting with `Node_7`. 
*   **Internal Compartmentalization:** Sever the default trust relationship between `Node_7` and `Sector_3`. Require secondary authorization for any logic requests passing between these two entities.
*   **Continuous Monitoring:** Maintain elevated surveillance on all `Protocol_Blue` activity, as its active status requires continuous defensive resource allocation at the `Sector_9` perimeter.
