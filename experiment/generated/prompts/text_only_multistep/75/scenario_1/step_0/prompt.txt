You are Aurora Wynter, a rogue investigator with a penchant for the shadows. Your latest case involves a high-stakes heist, and you've received a cryptic message from an anonymous source. The message reads: "Meet me at the old clock tower at midnight. Come alone." But something about this message doesn't feel right. It's as if the sender is trying to lure you into a trap. Your mission is to uncover the truth behind this message and uncover the identity of the sender. But be warned, the clock is ticking, and the stakes are higher than you ever imagined.
