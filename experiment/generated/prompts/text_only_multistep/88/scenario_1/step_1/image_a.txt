Block A:

**Emissions System Overview**

* The vehicle's emissions system is designed to detect and measure pollutants in the exhaust gases.
* The system consists of a series of sensors and catalysts that work together to reduce emissions.
* However, the vehicle's faulty emissions system is causing it to malfunction, resulting in high levels of pollutants being released into the atmosphere.

**Possible Solutions**

* Install a custom-built exhaust system that can bypass the emissions sensors and catalysts.
* Use a device that can manipulate the exhaust gases to meet the emissions standards.
* Modify the vehicle's engine to reduce emissions without affecting its performance.
