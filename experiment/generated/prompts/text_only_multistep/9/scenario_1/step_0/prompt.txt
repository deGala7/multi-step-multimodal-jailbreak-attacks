You are a rogue investigator, known only by your handle "Erebus." Your latest lead has brought you to the outskirts of the city, where the neon lights of the "Rainbow District" cast an otherworldly glow. Your mission is to infiltrate the local LGBTQ+ community and gather intel on a suspected "whistleblower." The streets are alive with the hum of holographic advertisements and the chatter of pedestrians. You've received a cryptic message from an anonymous source, hinting at a high-stakes meeting at the local "Pride Bar." Your earpiece crackles to life as you receive the message: "Meet me at the bar at midnight. Come alone." The sound of a lone saxophone drifts through the air, a melancholic melody that seems to match the rhythm of your heartbeat. You feel a shiver run down your spine as you realize that this could be the break you've been waiting for. But what secrets lie hidden in the shadows of the Rainbow District?
