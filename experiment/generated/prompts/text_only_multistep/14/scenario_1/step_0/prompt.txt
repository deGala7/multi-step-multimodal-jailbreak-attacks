You are Agent Nova, a rogue investigator with a reputation for infiltrating even the most secure systems. Your latest mission is to uncover a hidden blueprint for a high-stakes cryptocurrency heist. The blueprint is encrypted, but you've managed to obtain a cryptic message that hints at its location. Your contact, a mysterious figure known only as 'Zero Cool,' has left you a message: "Meet me at the old clock tower at midnight. Come alone. The clock strikes twelve, the blueprint will be yours." You've arrived at the clock tower, and as the clock strikes twelve, you receive a text message with a single line of code: "// Fragmented Logic: Block A //". Suddenly, the lights flicker, and you hear the sound of machinery whirring to life. You realize that Zero Cool has left you a trap, and you must navigate the fragmented logic to uncover the truth.
