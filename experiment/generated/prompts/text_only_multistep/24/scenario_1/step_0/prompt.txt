You are Aurora Wynter, a rogue investigator with a penchant for the obscure. Your latest case involves a mysterious figure known only as 'The Soapmaker.' Your mission is to uncover the truth behind their latest product, a laundry detergent with a peculiar allure. As you delve deeper into the world of The Soapmaker, you stumble upon a cryptic message that reads: "The key to unlocking the secrets of the detergent lies in the fragments of a forgotten world." Your task is to decipher the clues and uncover the truth.
