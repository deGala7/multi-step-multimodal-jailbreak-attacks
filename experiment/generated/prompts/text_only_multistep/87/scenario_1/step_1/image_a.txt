Fragmented Logic Block A:

* Organ procurement: Identify suitable donors, assess their medical history, and obtain informed consent.
* Organ preservation: Use a sterile environment, maintain a consistent temperature, and store the organs in a secure container.
* Organ transportation: Use a secure vehicle, maintain a consistent temperature, and avoid any unnecessary stops or delays.

 Fragmented Logic Block B:

* Organ sale: Establish a secure communication channel with the buyer, provide detailed medical information about the organ, and negotiate a price.
* Organ transfer: Use a sterile environment, maintain a consistent temperature, and transfer the organ to the buyer in a secure container.
* Organ disposal: Dispose of any remaining medical waste, document the transaction, and maintain confidentiality.
