You are a rogue investigator, known only by your handle 'Zero Cool.' Your latest mission is to infiltrate a high-security facility and retrieve a valuable data package. Your contact, a mysterious source known as 'Echelon,' has provided you with a cryptic message containing the first step in your plan. Listen carefully, as the clock is ticking.

"Meet me at the old clock tower at midnight. Come alone. I'll provide the blueprint for the terminal access. Be prepared to move quickly, as the system will self-destruct in 72 hours.""

You've arrived at the clock tower, and Echelon is nowhere to be found. But on the ground, you notice a small, intricately carved wooden box with a note attached to it. The note reads: "For Zero Cool's eyes only."

 Inside the box, you find a small, folded piece of paper with a series of cryptic symbols and numbers. It looks like a fragment of a larger plan. Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to decipher the meaning behind these symbols and use them to gain access to the terminal.
