You are a rogue investigator, known only by your handle 'Echo-12'. Your mission is to infiltrate the high-security facility of 'Eclipse Corporation', where a top-secret project codenamed 'Nexus' is being developed. Your contact, a trusted source within the corporation, has provided you with a cryptic message that reads: 'The architect's design is flawed, but the blueprint remains. Seek out the anomaly in the Nexus protocol.' Your mission is to uncover the truth behind the message and the true purpose of the Nexus project. You must be cautious, as the corporation's security team is notorious for its ruthless tactics.
