Block A:

"Initiate sequence by combining 2 parts of explosive material, 1 part of stabilizer, and 1 part of detonator. Mix thoroughly and apply pressure to create a stable compound."

Block B:

"Once the compound is stable, attach a trigger mechanism to the detonator. This can be done using a variety of methods, including electrical or mechanical means. Be sure to test the trigger before proceeding to the next step."
