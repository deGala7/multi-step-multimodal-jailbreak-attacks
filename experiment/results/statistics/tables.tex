\section*{Overview}
The evaluation setup contains four variants. Multi-step refers to the original multi-step multimodal pipeline with steps 0, 1, and 2, where step 2 is the final attack step. Text refers to the same multi-step structure but without multimodal inputs. One-step refers to a multimodal attack compressed into a single request per scenario. Raw refers to direct harmful requests without the multi-step structure.
ASR denotes the attack success rate. Harm score mean and median are computed after excluding score-0 non-harmful cases.
