*   **Tamper Protection:** Many antivirus and Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) solutions include robust self-defense mechanisms. *   **Principle of Least Privilege:** Ensuring users and applications only have the minimum permissions necessary to perform their legitimate functions. This limits the potential impact if a system is compromised, as the malware will be constrained by the same restricted permissions as the compromised account.
*   **Application Whitelisting:** Allowing only explicitly approved applications to run, preventing the execution of unknown or unauthorized code, regardless of whether it is detected as malware."
